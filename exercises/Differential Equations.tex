\subsection{Find the general solutions of the following differential equations.}

\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{enumerate}
\item $xy^2-y'x^2=0$
\item $y'+3x^2y=e^{-x^3}$
\item $y''+8y+16=0$
\item $y''+4y=0$
\item $y'=2x(1+y^2)$
\item $xy'+2y=x^2$, for $x>0$
\item $y''-5y'+6y=0$
\item $y''+6y'+9y=0$
\end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}


\subsection{Solve the following initial value problems.}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{enumerate}
\item $y'=xy,\quad y(0)=3$
\item $y'+2y=6,\quad y(0)=1$
\item $y''+9y=0,\quad y(0)=2,\quad y'(0)=-3$
\item $y'=2xy^2,\quad y(1)=1$
\item $y'=y(4-y),\quad y(0)=4$
\item $y'=y(4-y),\quad y(0)=1$
\item $xy'+2y=x,\quad y(1)=2$
\item $y''=6x,\quad y(1)=0,\quad y'(1)=2$
\item $y''-y=0,\quad y(0)=1,\quad y'(0)=0$
\item $y''+4y'+4y=0,\quad y(0)=1,\quad y'(0)=0$
\end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}

\subsection{Slope Fields and Euler's Method}
\begin{enumerate}
\item For $y'=x-y$, sketch the slope field at all nine points with $x,y\in\{0,1,2\}$. Through $(0,1)$, sketch the solution curve. Where does the field predict a horizontal tangent?
\item For the same equation with $y(0)=1$, use Euler's method with step size $0.5$ to estimate $y(1)$. Mark both steps on your sketch.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Word Problems}
\begin{enumerate}
\item If the half-life of a radioactive substance is 10 years, how long will it take to decay by 80\% assuming the rate of change of this substance is directly proportional to the amount present?
\item Coffee cools at a rate proportional to the difference between its temperature and the room temperature of $20^\circ$C. It cools from $80^\circ$C to $60^\circ$C in 5 minutes. How long after it was poured will it reach $40^\circ$C?
\item A pond has 100 fish. Its population $P$ follows the logistic model $P'=kP(1-P/500)$, where time is measured in years. Initially the population grows by 16 fish per year. Find $k$ and determine when the population will reach 250 fish.
\item A mass on a spring obeys $s''=-4s$, where $s$ is displacement from equilibrium in centimeters and time is in seconds. It is released from rest 6 cm above equilibrium. Taking upward as positive, find $s(t)$ and the first time the mass passes through equilibrium.
\item A tank initially holds 100 L of fresh water. Brine containing 3 g of salt per liter flows in at 2 L/min, and the well-stirred mixture flows out at the same rate. Write an initial value problem for the mass of salt in the tank. When will the concentration reach 2 g/L?
\item Water drains from a basin at a rate such that the water depth decreases in proportion to the square root of the depth. The depth drops from 36 cm to 25 cm in 2 minutes. Measured from the start, when will the basin be empty?
\item A rower stops rowing while her boat is moving at 4 m/s. Drag slows the boat at a rate proportional to its speed. After 5 seconds the speed is 2 m/s. If this model continues to hold, what total distance will the boat coast?
\item A festival needs a repair fund of \$10,000. The fund starts with \$2,000, earns interest continuously at 4\% per year, and receives donations at a constant rate of \$1,200 per year. Set up and solve an initial value problem to determine when the fund reaches its target.
\item A spherical hailstone melts at a rate such that its volume decreases in proportion to its surface area. Use $V=\tfrac43\pi r^3$ and $A=4\pi r^2$ to find how the radius changes with time. If the radius falls from 3 cm to 2 cm in 4 minutes, how much longer will the hailstone last?
\item In a meadow, rabbit and fox populations $R$ and $F$ satisfy $R'=0.6R-0.02RF$ and $F'=0.001RF-0.3F$, with time in months. Starting with 200 rabbits and 20 foxes, which population is increasing and which is decreasing? Find the positive population levels at which both rates would be zero.
\end{enumerate}
